## Who We Are

::lead The Journey of Grace (Mesa First) Church of the Nazarene was first organized on February 27, 1948 after several years of a small group of families praying for and asking to begin their own Nazarene church in Mesa. The church began with a small group of dedicated families and has stayed in the same neighborhood for over 60 years now. Through seasons of faith, prayer, and sacrificial giving, the church has grown and expanded its East University property.

We desire to be a neighborhood church that reaches out and impacts the community for the Kingdom of God. We exist to serve everyone created in God’s image, and to invite all to become disciples of Jesus. The church of the Nazarene has always desired to serve the marginalized, those on the outside, and those that are wondering about who God is. We desire to love like Jesus, grow closer to God and to each other, and to serve those who aren’t yet a part of our church family.

In 2021 our church board, along with our congregation, moved together to change the name of our church to Journey of Grace Church of the Nazarene to better reflect who we are as a congregation and our desire to invite those who are not here yet to allow us to journey along side them in their walk, extending the grace we all need.

We’d love to meet you, please feel free to check out more information about [visiting our church](visit) or [getting connected](connect) through our ministries.

## What We Believe

We are a member of the Church of the Nazarene, a global protestant denomination that is Wesleyan in its theology and came out of the American Holiness movement at the turn of the 20th century. The mission of the Church of the Nazarene is to, “Make Christlike Disciples in the Nations.”

::beliefs
- We Believe in God.
- We Believe in the inspiration and authority of scripture.
- We Believe in the Uniqueness of Jesus Christ.
- We Believe in Entire Sanctification.
- We Believe in the Church.

If you’d like to learn more about these beliefs, feel free to [visit our denominational page](https://nazarene.org/what-we-believe/) to read more in depth.
