## What Are Discipleship Groups?

Discipleship Groups meet weekly so we can grow as disciples of Jesus together — the way the early church did. We build our groups on the [five discipleship values](https://nazarene.org/resources/ndi-5-discipleship-values-en/) of authentic relationships, comprehensive biblical learning, fervent prayer, compassionate outreach, and intentional mentoring and equipping. In practice that looks like sharing life together, discussing the sermon and other biblical subjects, praying for one another, and spurring each other on to love and good works. Our goal is to get as many people as possible in our church into a Discipleship Group, where they can experience what it means to “do” life together, pray for, and support each other in Jesus.
