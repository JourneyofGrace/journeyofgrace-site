# Event & Facility Request

::lead Journey of Grace is glad to share our building and to help you get the word out about events in our church family. Whether you need a space to gather or would like an event posted on our website, this form is the place to start.

## What This Form Is For

Please fill out this form if you would like to utilize our facilities for an event and/or would like an event posted on the website. A member of our staff will review your request, confirm availability, and follow up with the details.

## What to Expect in the Form

The form asks for a few details so we can understand your request and plan carefully:

::beliefs
- Your name and the best way to contact you, such as an email address or phone number
- The name of your event or group, and a short description of what you have planned
- Your preferred date and time, along with any setup or facility needs you can think of
- Anything else that would help our staff prepare for your event

## What Happens Next

Once you submit your request, it goes straight to our office. We will review it, check the calendar, and get back to you within a few days to confirm the details and answer any questions.
