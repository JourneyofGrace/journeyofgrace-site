## Who We Are

Journey of Grace Kids Ministry includes children from birth through fifth grade, as well as a team of adult staff and volunteers devoted to helping kids learn to know, love, and trust in God with a faith that sticks. Some of that happens best through age-specific learning. On Sunday morning, our students learn to love Jesus through music, play, hands-on activities, and storytelling. Our team also feels very strongly about the benefit of intergenerational interaction and partnering with families. Our kids have the opportunity to learn alongside those further along in life through family worship, Family First Sunday lunches, as well as special events.

## How You Can Get Involved

It’s easy for kids and families to jump right in! There are classes going on every Sunday. Simply stop by our check-in desk just inside the Little’s Wing and we can help you get registered and find your group. We’re also always looking for more adults to join our team. Contact the [church office](mailto:office@journeyofgrace.church?subject=Children%27s%20Ministry) to find out more about the many positions available and the policies, training, and application process for becoming a volunteer.
