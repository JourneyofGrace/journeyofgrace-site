## What Are Discipleship Groups?

Discipleship Groups meet weekly and are focused around prayerful and personal fellowship. The groups come together, share in life together, pray together, and discuss the sermon and other Biblical subjects. Our goal is to get as many people as possible in our church into a Discipleship Group so that they can experience what it means to “do” life together, pray for, and support each other in Jesus.
