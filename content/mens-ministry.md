## Who We Are

Journey of Grace Men’s Ministry exists to build up men through fellowship, discipleship, service, and prayer.

## How You Can Get Involved

We meet every month for a prayer breakfast. We eat together, share struggles and victories together, and discuss how we can serve others together. There’s always work to be done, whether it’s helping someone in the church family move, helping with property care around the campus, or serving our neighbors.

There is also a weekly men’s Bible study on Fridays. Contact the *church office* for details.
