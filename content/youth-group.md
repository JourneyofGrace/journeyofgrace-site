## Who We Are

**ROC Student Ministries** exists to develop the youth of Journey of Grace Church of the Nazarene into lifelong disciples of Jesus that are connected to, and engaged in, the Kingdom of God and His Church.

## How You Can Get Involved

- We meet every Wednesday evening at 7:00 pm.
- Sunday Mornings, we meet for the Teen/Adult Journey Class from 9:00–10:00 am, where we have Bible Study.
